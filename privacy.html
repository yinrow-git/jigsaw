Privacy Policy for Jigsaw Tray

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Jigsaw Tray ("the app") is a jigsaw puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Summary

Jigsaw Tray does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no device or user identifiers. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not use cookies, device identifiers, or any technology designed to track you across apps or websites.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your device, using standard iOS storage — this data never leaves your device and is never sent to us:

  • Which puzzles you've already solved
  • Your preferred puzzle grid size
  • Puzzle images you've unlocked through a purchase (cached locally so they work offline)

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network Requests

To load puzzle images, the app makes requests to our server (jigsaw.up.railway.app) to fetch today's daily puzzle and additional puzzle images. These requests do not include any personal information or device identifiers — only what's needed to fetch the correct image (such as which puzzles you've already unlocked, described purely as a puzzle count).

In-App Purchases

Jigsaw Tray offers one optional in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store's standard purchase system. We never see or store your payment information — that's handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Jigsaw Tray does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
